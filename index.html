Dave the Divorce Doula

Supporting grief, clarity, and growth

This is one of the most painful and transformative experiences of your life. You are navigating difficult emotions while learning an unfamiliar process and making high-stakes decisions.

How you move through this now will shape your emotional well-being, finances, and quality of life for years to come. You deserve support during this relatively short but pivotal chapter.

I'm here for the whole journey: mourning the death of the life you thought you would have, finding your way through the valley of darkness, and recognizing the new life you are already creating.

Death

When a marriage ends, it’s important to grieve the loss of the relationship, your best friend, and the future you thought you would have.

Even if the decision to separate came as a surprise, the end of the marriage may have been long and painful. You may have spent years trying to save it, living with distance or conflict, and realizing how far the relationship had drifted from what you once hoped it would be.

You are also grieving a loss of identity.

Your home, family life, possessions, routines, financial stability, and the future you had been holding onto are all changing at once.

It is easy to get lost in anger, sadness, shame, blame, fear, lost confidence, and stories of failure that make the path through this loss even more painful and confusing.

Valley of Darkness

Separation throws you into painful and anxious uncertainty. You are juggling changes to housing, possessions, finances, legal decisions, communication with your ex, parenting, and the rhythms of everyday life. You may also be learning how to live with time away from your children, pets, and old routines.

It is difficult to find the support you need. Friends, family, lawyers, and therapists each offer an important perspective, but each of them only sees part of the picture. You are the one who has to weigh their advice and recognize when it is inaccurate, unhelpful, or shaped by someone else’s assumptions, fears, or unresolved experiences.

I’m there with you as you take in that larger picture, helping you find and stay connected to your inner compass. I’m able to offer you insights and perspective from my experience supporting other people through challenges across these areas, especially in those moments of uncertainty when different perspectives, fears, or parts of you are pulling in different directions.

Together, we identify the important decisions ahead, surface options you may not have considered, sort through competing advice, and weigh the tradeoffs of different paths.

Through this work, your own path becomes easier to see.

Rebirth

This is an amazing opportunity to understand the larger story of your life: who you were when this relationship began, how you grew through it, where that growth became limited, and what parts of you are asking to be cared for now.

I help you listen to these parts with curiosity and compassion. We look within feelings of anger, grief, and into painful stories to understand what they’re teaching you about your values and unmet needs, and how new boundaries can support your growth.

As your parts feel heard, cared for, and understood, the pain of old beliefs can begin to soften. Your responses become less reactive, more intentional, and more aligned with who you are choosing to be.

You are being born from the choices you make today.

A balanced approach

We begin by slowing down to check in. What feels heavy on your heart? What’s busy in your mind? What are you noticing in your body? I allow the conversation to flow naturally, while making sure we cover litigation, therapy, community, and self.

Litigation

The legal team you choose and the decisions you make with them will shape how difficult your divorce is right now and the impact it has on you in the years to come. Our conversations help you understand the process, evaluate your team, consider their advice, prepare for important meetings, and decide what will serve you best. I’m here to support your decision-making process, not to provide legal advice.

Therapy

The therapist you choose, the approach you take, and the work you prioritize all shape what you get from therapy. Our conversations help you reflect on what is happening between sessions, notice the deeper themes underneath the latest events, and decide what deserves more time with your therapist. I support the work you are doing in therapy without trying to replace it.

Community

The people you surround yourself with are your most valuable resource, especially as you begin a new life. Our conversations help you create the community you need for your interests, availability, and the ways you want to grow, and make sure you’re showing up to those relationships with openness, balance, and ease.

Self

The care and intention you bring to your relationship with yourself shape how you heal, grow, and experience the life you are creating. We may look at diet, exercise, sleep, routines, rituals, boundaries, self-compassion, and the ways you care for yourself when old fears or wounds are activated.

Benefits to you

Our weekly Zoom sessions help you stay grounded, get clear on what matters, make more informed decisions, and move forward with confidence.

Ease

You make confident, informed decisions from a grounded place. You spend less time reliving the past or fighting things you cannot change. Week by week, you feel calmer, clearer, and better able to appreciate the life ahead.

Efficiency

You get through the divorce faster, with greater confidence in the decisions you’ve made, while making meaningful progress on the personal growth and healing this experience has brought to the surface.

Savings

Our weekly Zoom call can easily cost less than an email from your lawyer. You reduce the cost of your divorce by using your legal team more efficiently and avoiding battles that do not serve you.
