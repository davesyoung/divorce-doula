A Grounded Practice

Holding space for the death of the old life—and the birth of the new.

Divorce is not just a legal dissolution; it is a profound identity transition. A Divorce Doula provides non-anxious presence, strategic triage, and compassionate advocacy through the emotional labor of starting over.

1. Legal Strategy & Advocacy

Save legal fees by processing emotional distress outside of $500/hour billable time. Arrive at mediation prepared, clear-headed, and aligned with your values.

2. Therapy & Parts Integration

Support for your clinical work. Utilizing frameworks like Internal Family Systems (IFS) to help you communicate with your parts and build inner contracts.

3. Community Triage

Navigating changing friendships, avoiding couple-circle politics, and consciously cultivating a social circle that reflects who you are becoming.

4. Self & Conscious Rebuilding

Anchoring the fundamentals: nervous-system regulation, nutrition, sleep routines, and stepping out of default patterns to design your next chapter.
